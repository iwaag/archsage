# archsage (retired)

**This instance is retired since 2026-10-16 and answers nothing.** Its
listener is stopped; mentions of `@**archsage**`, `sage:<name>` selectors
and posts in `{instance}` are no longer read, and the logical sages are gone.

archsage is now the **archsage** project of the workflow editor. It advises
from what the managed projects have learned (their study and wedo, read where
they live, with the revision read), performs requested studies, and
supplements a project's wedo only where that project assigned it:

- ask in the Zulip channel `wf-archsage.consult` (a `request-…` topic) for
  advice, `wf-archsage.study` for a study, `wf-archsage.freework` for anything
  else, or use *Request a run* on the archsage project in the workflow editor;
- reading any project needs no archsage at all: `wfe projects` (`wfe help
  projects`) is the system's own access to every managed project.

The sages' domains and queued questions were imported into the archsage
project's index with their provenance; the old conversations, the studies
(still autolab's) and the sage store stay as they are.
